## Overview

## Key Facts

- Provides support for new business locations or expansions in Georgia
- Offers a Site Selector platform with industrial and commercial properties listings
- Provides business recruitment services for companies new to Georgia
- Offers strategic growth plans and support to existing businesses
- Provides economic development services including engineering, research, and marketing
- Works closely with state and local partners to attract and expand businesses
- Offers statewide business recruitment and economic development services
- Provides specialized data and unmatched partnership to businesses
- Uses CoStar and REsimplifi subscriptions for data
- Lists buildings and sites in Georgia
- Provides research solutions including demographics, industry insights, and workforce analytics.
- Uses ESRI as the primary source for demographic information.
- Engineering team uses CAD technology, drone videography, and 3D modeling to visualize site developments in Georgia.
- Provides conceptual site layouts for maximizing land use.
- Provides complimentary services including digital 3D renderings, market research and demographics, custom maps, data reports, business stories and announcements.
- Serves as a liaison between state agencies and local communities.
- Publishes blogs about Georgia's economic development and logistics network.
- Highlights include investment totals, job creation numbers, and electric mobility ecosystem.
