# TotalEnergies

## Overview

## Key Facts

- The Board of Directors of TotalEnergies met on February 7, 2023 and decided to propose a dividend increase.
- TotalEnergies proposed an ordinary dividend of €2.81/share for the 2022 financial year.
- TotalEnergies is a global multi-energy company producing oil, natural gas, renewable energies, and electricity.
- TotalEnergies has over 100,000 employees.
- TotalEnergies redirects nearly $928 million from offshore wind development to natural gas projects.
- The company will return a 2022 offshore wind lease to the federal government and receive reimbursement.
- TotalEnergies will invest in US liquefied natural gas projects.
