# Port of Brownsville

## Overview

## Key Facts

- The Port of Brownsville is the largest land-owning public port authority in the nation.
- It has a Foreign-Trade Zone No. 62 which ranked No. 13 overall for the value of imports ($3.2 billion) and among the top three in terms of value of exported cargo ($7 billion).
