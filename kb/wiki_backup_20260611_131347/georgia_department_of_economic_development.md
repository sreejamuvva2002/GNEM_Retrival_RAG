## Overview

## Key Facts

- Pat Wilson is the longtime commissioner of Georgia Department of Economic Development (GDEcD)
- Governor Brian Kemp was the only U.S. governor at the World Economic Forum in Davos, Switzerland
- Announced $2 billion in foreign direct investment (FDI) in Georgia for fiscal year 2021.
- Twenty percent more jobs were created by FDI compared to the previous fiscal year.
