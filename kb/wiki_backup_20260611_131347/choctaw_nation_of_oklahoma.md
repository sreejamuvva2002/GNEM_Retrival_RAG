# Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma

## Overview

## Key Facts

- Choctaw Nation of Oklahoma (CNO) is third in terms of largest tribes in the United States with over 200,000 tribal citizens.
- The CNO was chosen by FAA and U.S. Department of Transportation as one of two locations to host an FAA-designated unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) test site.
