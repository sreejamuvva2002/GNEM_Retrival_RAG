- Materials on the website are protected by copyright and trademark law.

- Provides resources to help educate about reshoring
- Hosts the Innovation Garage podcast
- The Reshoring Initiative offers an Advanced Search feature for free to the public.
- The full Reshoring Initiative master database of over 6000 cases is available for private use for a fee.
- Offers sponsorship levels with benefits tailored to the type of organization and budget
- Provides services such as identifying and promoting cases of customers using sponsors' products for reshoring, joint articles and webinars, training sales teams on Total Cost comparison

- Provides custom data reports and consulting services.

- The organization lists sponsor companies and associations on its page
- Founded in early 2010 by Harry Moser
- Aims to help manufacturers realize the benefits of local production
- Maintains the industry’s leading database of U.S. reshoring and foreign direct investment (FDI) cases
- Provides unmatched visibility into where, why, and how companies are bringing production back to the United States
- Supports other groups dedicated to reshoring manufacturing jobs
- Lists related companies and organizations on its website
- Provides resources to educate about reshoring
- Has a website with survey information and analyst coverage
- The Reshoring Initiative helped bring over 338,000 manufacturing jobs back to the U.S. since 2010.

- The Reshoring Initiative is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization.

- Offers sponsorship levels for organizations to support its mission of bringing manufacturing back home
- Provides benefits such as identifying and promoting cases where companies are using their products in reshoring efforts
- Offers free tools and resources for manufacturers, economic developers, associations, and universities
- Provides a Total Cost of Ownership Estimator® tool
- Provides a free online tool called the Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Estimator
- The TCO Estimator helps companies account for all relevant factors including overhead, balance sheet, risks, corporate strategy and other external and internal business considerations
- Reshoring is driven by consumer demand for Made in USA products.
- Reshoring can mitigate risks associated with offshoring and lead to economic benefits.

- Provides consulting services to companies considering offshoring or reshoring
- Offers SCG (Supply Chain Gaps) and SBS (Selling and Buying Smarter) programs
- Compiles and stores reshoring trend data
